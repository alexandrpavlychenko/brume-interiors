Stages of work

  1. Consultation

    We discuss your needs and preferences, propose interior design solutions, and define the project timeline.

  2. Design Brief

    We prepare the design brief and formalize the agreement, clearly outlining every stage of the project.

  3. Measurements and Space Planning

    We carefully document the dimensions of walls, windows, baseboards, and doorways to ensure accurate planning and execution.

  4. Design Concept

    We create a design concept based on the client’s preferences, inspirations, and vision, carefully selecting stylistic and interior solutions.

  5. 3D Visualization

    We create detailed 3D visualizations and prepare technical drawings in accordance with construction standards.

  6. Interior Procurement

    We select furniture, finishing materials, lighting, and textiles, thoughtfully completing the interior with decorative details.
